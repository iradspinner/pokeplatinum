# Boss shapes

Each boss fight's shape: what makes it the fight it is, and what the player's plan against it rests on. This is how a change is judged for its effect on difficulty (Ian, 2026-10-07, the rule that heads CLAUDE.md). A change that touches a boss's core, or the player's core counterpiece to it, is material for that fight. A change to a one-off Pokemon on either side is not (Ian, 2026-10-12).

A shape is written when a boss's redesign passes its evidence and diagnosis stages (memory: boss redesigns in checked stages), and updated when the scorer's sixes show the player's answer has moved. Each entry says how sure it is.

## Roark (Oreburgh Gym, cap 16)

**Status:** redesigned 2026-10-12, sets being built by the Kaizo study. The scorer's comparison with today's team is pending.

**Identity.** A Rock gym in permanent sand, where the sand is the gym's weapon. Each member asks a different question, and the bait the player always brings has to be played around, not walked through.

**The boss's core:**
- Naclstack's Salt Cure, a quarter of max HP a turn on Water types, and Purifying Salt.
- Lileep's Storm Drain and its Mega Drain: the Water baits' attacks feed it, and Grass hits them.
- Sandshrew's Sand Rush in the permanent sand, with Poison Jab or X-Scissor for Grass baits.
- Anorith as the physical Grass-bait answer.
- Cranidos, the ace, with Rock Polish and Mold Breaker.
- The gym's permanent sand.

**The player's core counterpieces** (from the scorer's sixes):
- **Barboach**, in every six the scorer has taken to Roark. Water/Ground: both its attacking types hit Rock super-effectively, it resists Rock, Electric can't touch it, and sand doesn't hurt it.
- **Turtwig**, Bibarel and Dustox in most sixes; Fletchinder in some.

**Material for this fight:**
- Barboach's typing, its Water and Ground moves, or its availability before Roark.
- Turtwig's Grass moves.
- Salt Cure, Storm Drain or Sand Rush, in the engine or the simulator.
- Lileep's early Grass move.
- The moves or abilities of the five members.
- The gym's sand.

## Gardenia (Eterna Gym, cap 26)

**Status:** provisional, from a first look on 2026-10-12. The redesign starts after Roark's comparison.

**Identity.** The base ROM's team was a sun team: Cherrim sets 8 turns of sun, Chlorophyll members double their speed, Solar Beam fires in one turn, and Weather Ball turns to Fire. Its flaw is that sun doubles the player's Fire baits. The current draft dropped the sun and lost its identity. The redesign keeps the sun and fixes the flaw.

**The player's core counterpieces** (suspected, not yet read on today's box):
- **Fletchinder** (Fire/Flying): Grass hits it at a quarter, both its attacking types hit Grass super-effectively, and sun doubles its Fire. Rock hits it four times over.
- **Golbat** (Poison/Flying), the classic Grass-gym wall.

**Material for this fight:**
- Sun mechanics: Sunny Day, Chlorophyll, Weather Ball, Solar Beam.
- Fletchinder's and Golbat's availability, typing and moves.
- Rock moves on Gardenia's members.
