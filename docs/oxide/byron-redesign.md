## Stage 2: the diagnosis

Written as `boss-shapes.md` frames the other bosses. How sure: one scorer reading on three boxes, the traces and the calculator's figures.

The boss's core today is Heatran and Celesteela. Heatran is the only member that hurts the Steel walls, and Celesteela's one instant Meteor Beam makes the most faints. Bastiodon's Stone Edge on the Fire types and Dhelmise's Power Whip on the Water types are each a single answer that moves after its target.

The player's counterpiece is a class of walls. The Water types (Manaphy, Wailord, Milotic, Quagsire, Whiscash) and the Steel types (Corviknight, Bronzong, Metagross, Empoleon) take little from five of the six, since the team attacks mostly with Steel moves. Beside them sit fast Fire types (Cinderace, Houndoom, Rapidash, Delphox) and Ground types (Donphan, Hippowdon, Torterra) that hit four of the six hard. Corviknight was in all 20 of the screen's top sixes on boxes 1 and 3, and Wailord and Milotic in all 20 on boxes 2 and 3.

What a redesign must answer:

1. The Water walls. Two or more members with an Electric, Grass or Dragon hit that the Water types cannot hurt back, so the replacement rule can send one in.
2. The Steel walls. A second Fire, Ground or Fighting hit beside Heatran's.
3. The fast Fire types. A hit they fear from a member that survives their first hit, since no Steel type here outspeeds them: Rock, Ground or Water coverage, and Stealth Rock, which costs each of them a quarter of its HP on entry.
4. A lead with a job that lands. A Sash or bulk, so the hazard goes down before the lead falls, as Klefki's did not on box 2.
5. Celesteela's second Meteor Beam. Coverage the AI picks over a charging beam once the herb is gone.
6. Theme. Byron stays a Steel leader.

## How the numbers were made

Every number comes from this branch's worktree on the tree of 38981fc8, whose Byron file and balance tools match the reading's tree of 0db1aeaee. The scripts are in `/home/ian/.claude/jobs/byron-redesign/`, adapted from Cyrus 1's and run under `tools/oxide/capped`; none writes to `res/`.

- `walls.py` takes the likely answers from goal 3's fight state on each box (run name `byron-redesign-study`, seeds 1 to 3, level 53), with the four moves the reading's search picked for each.
- `sweep.py` runs the whole-Pokedex sweep through the calculator's own runner, and `rank.py` ranks it by need, leaving out the nearby bosses' species.
- `shape.py` swaps a whole party into Byron's file in memory, builds the reading's fight on each box, and prints each member's best hit on each answer and back from the scorer's own calculator rows. It runs `fightai.replacement` on a battle built by `perfectline.make_battle`, so the replacement picks are the rule's own, not worked by hand, and it prints the matchup screen's top sixes. `shapes.py` holds the parties; `digest.py` and `trace.py` read the output.
- `legal.py` checks each move against the Kaizo study's checker, with the TMs from before the TM pass.

The cheap probe was not run. It read today's team at 41 to 86 rough wins of 150 against the scorer's 100 percent, so in this range it cannot rank shapes (`probe-ranking.md`). No scorer reading was run and the simulator was not touched.
