# Cyrus 2's build: the end of this world

Cyrus 2 is the Galactic HQ fight, his ace at 59, just before Saturn 2 at 60. This is the primary team, built on Ian's free hand of 2026-11-04 and erring on the side of too hard, aimed at 80 to 90 percent won with three or more faints a fight on the scorer. It is saved in his file on the branch `overseer-cyrus2-build`. Nothing on this page has been played in game, and the scorer has not read it: the full reading on goal 3's three boxes is the Overseer's, and it decides where the fight lands. The cheap checks put it in the same band as Cyrus 1, which the scorer read at 82 percent won. Ian answered the page's four questions on 2026-11-04 (below): he keeps the six, Hypnosis and the three legendaries, and Deoxys takes Psycho Boost in place of Psychic.

## Why each set is as it is

Kaizo's HQ Cyrus, for reference, fields Palkia, Dialga, Deoxys-Defense (Psycho Boost, Superpower, Pursuit, ExtremeSpeed), Weavile (Triple Axel, Close Combat, Avalanche, Pursuit), Heatran and Regigigas, at 92 to 97 in a permanent Trick Room. The build keeps his structure: a Weavile ace with Pursuit, a Deoxys, and the gods he means to bend. Translated into Oxide's Generation 5 and later pool: Triple Axel and Technician, Oblivion Wing, Fiery Wrath, Dark Aura, Beast Boost, the Expert Belt and the Life Orb. Scaled to Oxide's difficulty: one 680 legendary in place of Dialga and Palkia, Deoxys's Attack Forme in place of its Normal Forme (680 on Oxide's stats), no ExtremeSpeed, no Trick Room, and levels of 57 to 59. Heatran stays with Cyrus 3, which carries it now, and Regigigas (670 on Oxide's stats) was left out.

Deoxys leads because it outspeeds the whole box and Magic Guard makes its Sash certain. Hasty keeps its Attack whole for Drain Punch. Superpower was tried first and reads worse for the AI: it sits in Evaluate Attack's no-calculation table and Expert docks it when the user is faster, so Drain Punch, which the AI weighs normally, takes its place. Psycho Boost is Ian's choice over Psychic (2026-11-04). Expert scores it as it scores Overheat, a point lower when the target resists or when the user is faster and at 60 percent HP or less, and the simulator applies the two-stage drop (`fightsim.HIT_SELF_STAGES`, the Overheat effect; a test battle on box 1 read Sp. Atk at minus 2 after one use and minus 4 after two). The AI used it 120 to 182 times in each 150 probe fights.

Tyranitar is the team's least productive member by knockouts (9 to 99 in 150 fights), and stays for what it draws. The Fire and Electric answers it pulls in meet Deoxys, Xurkitree and Yveltal next. The Chople Berry is for its four-times Fighting weakness.

Houndoom keeps the placeholder's Nasty Plot and Sludge Bomb, with Fire Blast and Fiery Wrath, its level 56 move, for more power. Flash Fire absorbs the Fire moves aimed at Weavile, and Timid puts it level with Cinderace, at 156.

Xurkitree is Modest, so Beast Boost raises its Sp. Atk, the stat Modest makes its highest. It made the most knockouts on every box in the probe (236 to 677 in 150 fights).

Yveltal is Mild, since Modest is Xurkitree's. Roost replaced Sucker Punch, which the AI rarely uses on these flags: Sucker Punch is in the same no-calculation table as Superpower.

Weavile keeps the placeholder's ace seat at the cap. Pursuit is on Sneasel's egg list already; Triple Axel is its level 51 move and Ice Shard its level 49 move.

The canon picks spread this way: Honchkrow on Cyrus 1, Weavile and Houndoom here, Gyarados on Cyrus 3. Crobat, the fifth, is Mars's bat on her three later teams, so it stays off.

| Weavile, 59 | Triple Axel (level 51), Pursuit (Sneasel's egg list), Brick Break (TM 31), Ice Shard (level 49) |

Psycho Boost is on no Deoxys list, so it went on the Attack Forme's egg list by hand: the `learnset.egg_moves` field of `res/pokemon/deoxys/forms/attack/data.json`, a field that record did not have, written in the files' style after `by_tutor`. The OxiDex's check reads a form's own record, and it finds Psycho Boost by egg at 57 for the Attack Forme and not for the Normal Forme. It is not in `learnrewrite.IAN_EGG_MOVES`, which cannot carry a form: the list is keyed by species constant, and the generator writes each entry into the base species' file (`pokedex.folder_of`), here `res/pokemon/deoxys/data.json`, the Normal Forme. The generator never writes a form's file, so it cannot undo the hand edit either.

The ROM's build had never met an egg list on a form record. Its species processor (`tools/dataproc/src/speciesproc.c`, `emit_eggmoves`) wrote every record's egg list into the game's egg move table, which is keyed by species, and named the form's entry by its folder (`SPECIES_DEOXYS/FORMS/ATTACK`), so the build failed. The processor now skips the alternate forms' records, as its footprint step already does, since a form's egg list is the trainers' palette and no part of the game's table. The generated table is identical to the one before, less the form's block.
