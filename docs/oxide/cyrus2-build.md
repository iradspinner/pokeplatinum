# Cyrus 2's build: the end of this world

Cyrus 2 is the Galactic HQ fight, his ace at 59, just before Saturn 2 at 60. This is the primary team, built on Ian's free hand of 2026-11-04 and erring on the side of too hard, aimed at 80 to 90 percent won with three or more faints a fight on the scorer. It is saved in his file on the branch `overseer-cyrus2-build`. Nothing on this page has been played in game, and the scorer has not read it: the full reading on goal 3's three boxes is the Overseer's, and it decides where the fight lands. The cheap checks put it in the same band as Cyrus 1, which the scorer read at 82 percent won.

## Why each set is as it is

Kaizo's HQ Cyrus, for reference, fields Palkia, Dialga, Deoxys-Defense (Psycho Boost, Superpower, Pursuit, ExtremeSpeed), Weavile (Triple Axel, Close Combat, Avalanche, Pursuit), Heatran and Regigigas, at 92 to 97 in a permanent Trick Room. The build keeps his structure: a Weavile ace with Pursuit, a Deoxys, and the gods he means to bend. Translated into Oxide's Generation 5 and later pool: Triple Axel and Technician, Oblivion Wing, Fiery Wrath, Dark Aura, Beast Boost, the Expert Belt and the Life Orb. Scaled to Oxide's difficulty: one 680 legendary in place of Dialga and Palkia, Deoxys's Attack Forme in place of its Normal Forme (680 on Oxide's stats), Psychic in place of Psycho Boost, no ExtremeSpeed, no Trick Room, and levels of 57 to 59. Heatran stays with Cyrus 3, which carries it now, and Regigigas (670 on Oxide's stats) was left out.

Deoxys leads because it outspeeds the whole box and Magic Guard makes its Sash certain. Hasty keeps its Attack whole for Drain Punch. Superpower and Psycho Boost were tried first, and both read worse for the AI: Superpower sits in Evaluate Attack's no-calculation table and Expert docks it when the user is faster, so Drain Punch, which the AI weighs normally, takes its place.

Tyranitar is the team's least productive member by knockouts (9 to 99 in 150 fights), and stays for what it draws. The Fire and Electric answers it pulls in meet Deoxys, Xurkitree and Yveltal next. The Chople Berry is for its four-times Fighting weakness.

Houndoom keeps the placeholder's Nasty Plot and Sludge Bomb, with Fire Blast and Fiery Wrath, its level 56 move, for more power. Flash Fire absorbs the Fire moves aimed at Weavile, and Timid puts it level with Cinderace, at 156.

Xurkitree is Modest, so Beast Boost raises its Sp. Atk, the stat Modest makes its highest. It made the most knockouts on every box in the probe (236 to 677 in 150 fights).

Yveltal is Mild, since Modest is Xurkitree's. Roost replaced Sucker Punch, which the AI rarely uses on these flags: Sucker Punch is in the same no-calculation table as Superpower.

Weavile keeps the placeholder's ace seat at the cap. Pursuit is on Sneasel's egg list already; Triple Axel is its level 51 move and Ice Shard its level 49 move.

The canon picks spread this way: Honchkrow on Cyrus 1, Weavile and Houndoom here, Gyarados on Cyrus 3. Crobat, the fifth, is Mars's bat on her three later teams, so it stays off.

### Species nearby

No member repeats a species from Cyrus 1 (Swampert, Honchkrow, Porygon-Z, Electivire, Magmortar, Salamence) or from Cyrus 3 as it stands (Dusknoir, Gyarados, Heatran, Flygon, Regirock, Salamence), so this build has no overlap with Cyrus 3. Cyrus 3 shares Salamence with Cyrus 1, which the Scheduled entry already drops.

The placeholder's Magnezone and Dusknoir left partly because the HQ already holds them or their type. Scientist Darrius fields a Magnezone at 57 in the HQ, and the HQ's officers and grunts carry nine Ghost types (Banette, Sableye, Cofagrigus, Polteageist, Spiritomb, Gengar and two Mismagius, with Runerigus). The officer teams of the HQ (Phobos and Deimos, Boreas and Laverna, Argo, Hesperid), Saturn 2 and Mars 2 share none of the six. Darkrai, a natural fit, is Officer Somnu's signature on Mt. Coronet 5F, the fight after the HQ, and Byron already holds Magearna, Kartana and Celesteela, so none of them was used. Weavile and Houndoom also stand on Lake Verity's Grunt 2 at 52, beside a Honchkrow, as a nod to Cyrus. Deoxys, Xurkitree and Yveltal stand on no other trainer before the post-game.
