## Summary

Deimos's theme is "dread slows": his side lowers how fast the player's side moves. Galvantula did that with a Sticky Web behind a Focus Sash, and the AI laid the Web in nearly every fight. Byron's Galvantula now carries the same Web a few fights later, so the replacement should slow the player some other way, or at least on another species.

The options, each at 47 with the other four members and Deimos's flags unchanged (Basic, Evaluate Attack, Expert, Check HP, Harassment):

My recommendation is Jolteon. Thunder Wave does its slowing: it is in the Harassment table Deimos already carries, and the AI chose it on about two turns of every five that Jolteon spent on the field. It keeps Galvantula's Electric hit and outspeeds every Pokemon of all three boxes. It carries no Web, so nothing repeats Byron's Galvantula. And it reads the hardest of the four on the doubles stand-in below. Its cost is that paralysis lands on one target, where the Web slowed every Pokemon the player's side sent in. Volkner fields a Jolteon too, much later and at 67.

Weavile is the Galactic choice, Cyrus's own Pokemon. But the AI almost never chooses its Icy Wind (52 times in 900 fights), so in play it slows nothing. It also adds a third Dark or Ice type beside Absol and Froslass. Leavanny keeps the Web, which the AI lays as reliably as Galvantula's, but it does not settle the repeat with Byron, only moves it to another species.

Each option breaks the line from Jubilife: Deimos's Galvantula grew out of his Joltik there, as most Pal Park members grow out of a Jubilife one. The new lead stands as a new member, as Absol and Froslass do. Changing Jubilife's Joltik as well would be a question of its own; an Eevee there would clash with the partner's Eevee lead.

What could not be checked, first. No scorer reading can read a tag battle, and the doubles stand-in has never been held against a reading. The harness cannot field the player's partner either, so the full tag battle (the box's six against both officers' ten) floors out: the stand-in won 0 to 8 of 300 a box against every lead, and the numbers rank nothing. The comparison below plays Deimos's five alone as a double against the box's six, the Somnu and Moira check's stand-in on the simulator's own double battle. Only the differences between rows mean anything. On the singles probe a gap under about 35 wins has not ordered two fights reliably, and nothing that strong is known for this proxy, so only the larger gaps count.

Why slowing matters here. On box 1, one Speed stage down moves the catches that Feraligatr outspeeds from 35 to 55 of 55, Arbok's from 35 to 54, Masquerain's from 26 to 49, and Granbull's from 9 to 29. Paralysis halves Speed, which is more than a stage, but on one target.

## How the numbers were made

The scripts are in `/home/ian/.claude/jobs/382760ce/tmp/deimos-lead/`, and none writes to `res/`. `sm.py` is the Somnu and Moira check's harness, pointed at this branch's worktree and at goal 3's box for Barry 5 (run `deimos-lead-study`, seeds 1 to 3, level 50), since Pal Park sits in Barry 5's stretch. `CAND` swaps a lead into Deimos's slot in memory. `dfights.py` plays `run_doubles` with the trainer side on `fightai.choose_doubles` and the player on the stand-in, which picks the attack worth the most or a major status, never switches and never sets up. `lead.py` reads the calculator's rows and the Speed counts. `runall.sh` ran the jobs under `tools/oxide/capped`. The candidate files are in the same folder as `cand-<name>.json`.
