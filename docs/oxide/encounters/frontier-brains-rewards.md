# Thorton's encounter and Argenta's reward: proposals for Ian

Written 2026-09-27 by the encounter track, for the Frontier Brains entry in
the tracker's Phase 5 (Ian, 2026-09-27). Both places are in Byron's split,
whose level cap is 53. Neither proposal uses a legendary, since legendary-tier
encounters are held back while the difficulty is low (Ian, 2026-09-27). The
worth figures are the box simulator's, 0 to 100, for the stage a line reaches
by Byron's cap; Ian's scarcity rule watches 85 and above.

## Rulings

Ian, 2026-09-27, relayed by the Overseer:

- **Thorton:** the player picks one of the two starter lines they did not
  choose (of Turtwig, Scorbunny and Piplup), fully evolved at level 40, as a
  Battle Factory rental. It shares the Fuego Ironworks capture with the yard.
  The box simulator's measurement follows the balance track's rescore.
- **Argenta:** undecided between items and a level-40 static; both stay open.

The proposals as written are below, for the record.

## Thorton, at the heart of Fuego Ironworks

The building has no table of its own, but it carries the location name
"Fuego Ironworks", the same as the yard outside, and the yard's table is in
Byron's split too. So a scripted encounter at the heart shares the yard's one
nuzlocke capture. That makes Thorton's prize a choice rather than an extra
Pokemon: the player takes the prize or a catch from the yard, whose best
lines are Magnezone (82.1), Magmortar (76.1) and Chandelure (74.2). Giving the
heart a location name of its own would make the prize a capture on top; I
recommend the shared capture, because it adds nothing to a box's size.

**Recommended: Cinderace, level 40, a static battle.** The Scorbunny line is
one of Ian's super-wanted lines and today its only source is the starter
choice, so a run that picks Turtwig or Piplup never sees it. A second source
at a forge suits a Fire line, and it follows the pattern the classic starters
already have (each keeps a scripted source; a starter is gate tier, not
legendary tier). Cinderace is worth 83.0, under the 85 line. Level 40 follows
the statics before it, which sit well under their split's cap (Rotom at 20
under 33, Spiritomb at 25 under 38). A player who chose Scorbunny meets a
duplicate, which the dupes clause lets them skip in favour of the yard.

What it should do to the box simulator, before measuring: in a run whose
starter is not Scorbunny, the Fuego Ironworks capture moves from a Magnezone,
Magmortar or Chandelure to Cinderace, so the box's worth barely
changes, one more wanted line lands, and no line at 85 or more is added. The
measured run follows after the balance track's rescore.

**The alternative: a Battle Factory rental, drawn at random.** Thorton runs
the Factory, where every Pokemon is a rental, so the heart could hold one line
drawn from a pool, as Riley's egg does. The pool would be the lines that today
appear only as one or two cameo slots: Abra (Alakazam, 71.1), Happiny
(Blissey, 74.8), Lickitung (Lickilicky, 71.6), Tropius (68.1), Girafarig
(64.2), Mawile (42.8) and Smoochum (Jynx, 55.0). It gives seven thin lines a
real source, is worth less on average than the yard, and so would be taken
mostly when the dupes clause has emptied the yard.

## Argenta, on Pal Park's second floor

Pal Park has no table, so anything caught there is a capture of its own, and
an egg counts where it hatches. Either way a Pokemon reward adds one capture
to Byron's split, which already has two scripted ones (the Canalave Library
gift and Riley's egg). An item reward adds none.

1. **Items, recommended if the box should not grow.** One option is the fixed
   Sun Stone and Moon Stone finds Ian ruled for Espeon and Umbreon on
   2026-09-27, which the balance track's stone census places; Argenta would be
   one place to put them, at the cost of holding those two Eeveelutions back to
   Byron's split. Held items that change a fight (Leftovers, Life Orb,
   Eviolite, Assault Vest) are the other kind, and the balance track's item
   pass decides which the game has. No Choice item, by Ian's ruling.
2. **An egg of a baby line, recommended if the reward should be a Pokemon.**
   Argenta's Battle Hall is one Pokemon against every type, and an egg drawn
   from several baby lines fits it. The thinly sourced babies are Happiny
   (Blissey, 74.8, one cameo slot), Smoochum (Jynx, 55.0, two cameo slots) and
   Elekid (Electivire, 76.1, today only the Veilstone gift); Mime Jr. has a
   Trophy Garden home and is left out. All three hatch at level 1 and reach
   their final stage under Byron's cap with the Pocket PC's Rare Candies, and
   none passes 85. The one catch is that it is the split's second random egg,
   after Riley's.
3. **A static at Pal Park**, level 40, from the same thin lines. It suits Pal
   Park's old role as the place Pokemon from other regions turn up, with
   Tropius or Lickitung as the obvious picks, but a static and an egg do the
   same thing to a box, and the egg carries the Battle Hall's theme better.

If Ian takes the rental pool for Thorton and an egg for Argenta, the two
pools should not share a line, so that neither prize is a duplicate of the
other.
