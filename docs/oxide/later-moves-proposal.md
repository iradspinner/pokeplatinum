Written by `laterlearn.py` (2026-09-27) for the Overseer to read before Ian. For each of the 335 of vanilla Platinum's species that Oxide keeps obtainable, the moves from Generation 5 on that a later game teaches by level-up (Black and White to Legends Z-A, hg-engine's per-game lists), each proposed through the learnset generator's rules on top of the learnset proposal. Nothing here is in the game data. `docs/oxide/later-moves-proposal.tsv` has every row with the games and the reasons; `docs/oxide/later-moves-tm.tsv` has the later moves those games give only by TM, tutor or egg, for the TM pass.
