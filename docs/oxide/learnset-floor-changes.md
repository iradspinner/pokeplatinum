# What Kaizo's own level moved in the learnset proposal

Ian ruled on 2026-09-27 that a strong move's translated placement is never earlier than Kaizo's own level (for a species Kaizo lacks, the level its nearest Kaizo lines learn the move at), but never past the end of the Oxide split that level translates to. This lists the 248 entries of the proposal (`docs/oxide/learnset-proposal.md`) whose level that rule changed, against the proposal as it stood before the rule (8338189c9). An entry the one-level rule then moved shows its final place. Ian's five early moves (Spiritomb's Dark Pulse and Vikavolt's Discharge among them) follow their own ruling. Nothing here is in the game data.

How far they moved:

| Change | Entries |
|---|---|
| 1 or 2 levels later | 121 |
| 3 to 5 later | 94 |
| 6 to 10 later | 31 |
| 11 or more later | 0 |
| Earlier | 2 |

The 2 shown earlier sit at their translated place: the proposal before had them a level or two later only through the one-level rule, and the floor's split had no free level at or above it, so the one-level rule now keeps them in that split, never under the translated place.
