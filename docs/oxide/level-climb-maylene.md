# The level climb in Maylene's split

Ian asked on 2026-10-29 for the ordinary trainers' levels to climb in play order through each split, never at the cap, very rarely one under it, with harder and less textbook sets at lower levels. This page is that climb for Maylene's split (after Fantina, cap 33, to Maylene, cap 39), on the branch `overseer-climb-maylene`.

## Summary

The 36 ordinary teams of the split now climb from 33 on Route 209 to 37 on Route 215 and in the Game Corner, with one trainer at 38 and none at 39. Every one of them was rebuilt or retuned, Twins Emma and Lil apart (their levels only). 44 species came in and 36 of them are from after Platinum, so 36 of the 116 members of the rebuilt teams are now later species, where before there was one Mienshao. Natures moved off the textbook: Adamant, Timid, Jolly and Modest went from 57 members to one Adamant, and no member is left without a named nature. Every changed team passed the trainer lint and the real packer. No boss and no hand-designed team was touched.

**For Ian to decide.**

1. Barry 3 has no honour cap, and one would help the climb. He is the split's first fight, at the gate onto Route 209, with his ace at 39. A player who candies to 39 for him meets every later trainer at the cap, and any experience past 39 benches that Pokemon until Wake's split. An honour cap of 35, with his ace at 35, would start the split where the climb starts.
2. Twins Emma and Lil kept their five unnamed natures by name. Raising their levels by two would have re-rolled them (the game derives an unnamed nature from the level), and two would then have repeated. They are pinned to the natures the team had: Rash, Bold, Hardy, Hasty and Docile, with Ian's Modest Cacturne.
3. Look at the teams below as a set before the branch lands. Most of them are new.

**Unverified.**

- No fight was read by the scorer. The cheap checks below are the only measure, and they read the box at the cap (39). The soft-cap box for a mid-split fight will sit lower, so every margin here is a little kinder to the player than the fight will be.
- The six double battles were checked as singles.
- No team has been seen in game. The real packer accepted each.
- Play order comes from the zone order and each trainer's tile, since `plgoal3 --list` lists only the bosses and Ace Trainers. The gym's order is read from where its trainers stand, not walked.
- Teeter Dance and Dynamic Punch are illegal until the egg lists gain them.
- The trainer AI does not see several of the held items these teams carry (Absorb Bulb, Weakness Policy, Rocky Helmet, Loaded Dice, the tracker's battle AI item 3). The engine runs them; the AI's choices ignore them.
- The calculator did not resolve Faint Attack (Kirby's Weavile) or Hi Jump Kick (Nathaniel's Hitmonlee, Colby's Riolu), so those were not damage-checked.

**Next steps.** The Overseer adds the two egg moves and lands the branch with `merge-branch.sh` (about 30 minutes with the build and the gate). Ian answers the Barry 3 question. No scorer reading is planned, under the standing ruling.
