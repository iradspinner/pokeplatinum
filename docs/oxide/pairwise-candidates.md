# Pairwise candidates for the scoring rebuild

These 40 pairs are the test the rebuilt fight score has to pass (Ian, 2026-09-27). The
balance track drafted them; Ian judges each one. Today's scores are left out on
purpose, so that his judgements stay his own.

Ian answered all forty on 2026-09-27, each naming the harder fight for a player at that
split's level cap and how much harder, in six grades from least to greatest: the same,
very close, a bit, a lot, massive, comical (pair 16's "a little" is his word, read as a
bit). Where a pair's trainers fight as doubles in the game, he judged them as singles.
Each name opens that trainer's team in the encounter tool's team builder, and a story
fight with a team for each starter links every team.

Twenty-five pairs fit the new score's weights. The other fifteen are held out and
never seen by the fit, and the rebuilt score replaces today's only when it agrees
with Ian on at least 13 of them: it names the same fight harder, and for "the same" and
"very close" it puts the two within the margin the fit sets. The held-out pairs are numbers 2, 5, 8, 11, 15, 18, 19, 22, 23, 25, 28, 31, 32, 35 and 38.

Every split has at least two pairs. In 16 of them a team that leans on
status or setup faces a plain hard-hitting one, since those are the fights today's
score misreads: numbers 4, 5, 7, 10, 11, 14, 15, 17, 19, 21, 25, 26, 28, 30, 33 and 38.
