Argenta's team is in the tree but she is not yet placed on `oxide` (a helper is placing her), so she is ranked but named apart.

## How the numbers were made

Every number comes from the worktree `.claude/worktrees/probe-ranking`, on the tree of 61b9d0391. The scripts are the Byron and Brains check's, as Barry 5 and Riley's check left them, copied to `/home/ian/.claude/jobs/probe-ranking/` and pointed at this worktree; none writes to `res/`. `bc.py` gains Dahlia's slot (dummy_007), stands each box at the honour caps above, and can swap in a whole trainer file from a past reading's tree (for the three earlier teams, from `~/oxide-trials/goal3/tree-galactic-1`) or stand one place's box at another level. `checks.py FIGHT PLACE SEED` runs the screen and the probe as before: 150 rough fights against the best and the most-taken six, with the greedy probe player of the Maylene and Wake checks, who takes its hardest hit by the lowest roll 85 percent of the time and otherwise a random action, and never switches, sets up or holds an item. `run.sh`, run under `tools/oxide/capped`, ran the 132 jobs, six at a time; `gather.py` and `table.py` made the table. The boxes come from `plgoal3.setup` with the fresh run name `probe-ranking-study`, seeds 1 to 3, so no box carries an earlier reading's choices, as in the earlier checks.

The probe reproduces the earlier checks exactly where nothing has changed since: Wake, Barry 4, Byron, Thorton on both sets of boxes, Cyrus 1 as softened, Riley, and Argo 1 at 47 (the earlier check's 99, 110, 53, 71 and 70). Barry 5 moved a little (79, 20; 105, 97; 17, 31 before), since his Serperior's Miracle Seed landed after his check. One of Argenta's cells moved, her box 2 most-taken six from 63 to 54, with her file unchanged since that check; the cause was not traced.

Where the probe's bias bites, as the calibration shows: the greedy player never switches against a setup move, never plays round status and never uses its own, so a fight whose danger is status, stall or a sweep reads harder than a careful player finds it, and the more so in a long fight. Hazards bite only after a knockout.
