# Simulator gaps

This lists every move effect, ability and held item that Oxide's fight simulator does not run, or runs differently from Oxide's engine, as the scorer's readings meet them. It was checked on 2026-10-13 against origin/oxide at 93ad737260, with the simulator frozen: nothing in it was changed.

## Summary

Every move in res/moves was checked through its battle effect (380 effects over 923 moves), with every ability in any species' slots (194) and every held item a trainer holds or the player can be handed (75). Each was read on the readings' own path, which is perfectline's turn (its _use_move, attack, damage_of and status_move) with fightsim's switch-in and turn's end, the trainer AI and the calculator's rows. Each was then compared with Oxide's engine in src/battle and res/battle/scripts, and a small test battle settled every case where the right behaviour or the simulator's was unclear. Double battles run on fightsim's own battle instead and have a section of their own.

| Kind | Checked | Runs right | Runs approximately | Does not run | Runs wrongly | Gaps a trainer meets (in a boss fight) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Move effects | 380 | 187 | 45 | 108 | 40 | 99 (33) |
| Abilities | 194 | 97 | 25 | 58 | 14 | 51 (33) |
| Held items | 75 | 69 | 1 | 4 | 1 | 6 (3) |

Ian approved six fixes on 2026-10-13, and they are being built on overseer-sim-fixes-2. They cover Future Sight and Doom Desire's delay, Sleep Talk calling Rest, Disable and Nightmare, Intimidate's four blockers (Inner Focus, Own Tempo, Oblivious, Scrappy) together with two-hit moves against Sturdy and the Focus Sash, Intimidate's double count and Unburden's double count. The tables mark them. Everything else is open for Ian to decide.

Next steps are Ian's. He decides which open gaps to fix, since each is a simulator change and needs his approval one by one. The small fixes take about an hour each with their tests. Roost, Encore and Baton Pass take about half a day each, the double battles' path about a day. A fix stales only the fights it moves, which are the fights beside it above and in the tables below, so only those need a new reading. Until then a reading of a fight that meets an open gap carries its error.

Each table row gives the mechanic, its verdict, what Oxide's engine does, what the readings' path does instead, the trainer teams that use it (with the boss fights among them), what the player can have, the evidence and what fixing it would take. Rows run from the most boss fights down, then by teams, then by the player's species. The counts are over the 768 trainer files the scorer reads, on origin/oxide; the 160 it does not read (159 dummy files and the empty none file) are left out. So the drafted Galactic teams on overseer-galactic-teams are not in the counts, and their own check closes this doc.

## Gaps that cut across many mechanics

A few gaps sit in code that many moves, abilities and items share, so they are listed once here and pointed to from the tables below.

| Gap | Oxide's engine | The simulator | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| The readings' damage lacks two of fightsim's own corrections | the engine applies Flash Fire's boost only once a Fire move has lit it (BATTLEMON_FLASH_FIRE), and lets Brick Break's kind and an Infiltrator pass Reflect and Light Screen (battle_lib.c 8638) | fightsim's Battle.damage has both corrections, but perfectline.damage_of, which every singles reading uses, has neither: the calculator rows a Flash Fire attacker as always lit, so its Fire moves land at 1.5 times before anything has lit it, and a Brick Break into Reflect is halved (15 against 30 in a test) before the screen is broken | Flash Fire, REMOVE_SCREENS and Infiltrator rows |
| PP on charging and locked turns, and Pressure | PP is taken once, on the turn the move is chosen: a battler held in a charge or a rampage cannot pick a command, and its turn is marked as already paid (battle_controller_player.c 336); each Pressure holder on the other side adds one (2402) | perfectline._use_move takes one PP every turn, so the charge turn of Fly, Dig, Dive, Bounce, Solar Beam and Skull Bash and each locked turn of Outrage, Thrash and Rollout cost one more; Pressure costs nothing extra | FLY, CHARGE_TURN_DEF_UP, CHARGE_TURN_HIGH_CRIT, Pressure |

## Double battles

The three tag boss fights (Somnu and Moira, Mars and Jupiter, Flint and Volkner) and the 18 ordinary double battles are read through pdoubles, which plays fightsim's own four-Pokemon battle (run_doubles) with fightsim's own attack and status_move rather than perfectline's. That path lacks several things perfectline's has, each checked with a hand-built battle on fightsim.attack and fightsim.status_move:

| Mechanic | Oxide's engine | fightsim's own battle | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sitrus, Oran and the pinch berries on a hit | eaten the moment the hit takes HP to the threshold | not checked after a hit: Sitrus waits for the turn's end, the pinch berries and Oran fire only after residual damage | a Sitrus holder hit from 100 to 40 kept its berry (perfectline: 65, berry eaten) |
| Mean Look, Block, Spider Web, Ingrain | trap the target, root the user | do nothing: fightsim.status_move has no branch for PREVENT_ESCAPE or GROUND_TRAP_USER_CONTINUOUS_HEAL | trapped_by stayed None, ingrained False (perfectline: both set) |
| Quick Claw, Custap Berry | a holder that fires moves first in its bracket | run_doubles orders by priority and Speed alone | run_doubles names neither |
| Scope Lens, Razor Claw | one critical stage | no stage | 3.5% critical hits with a Scope Lens (perfectline 13.3%) |
| King's Rock, Razor Fang | 10% flinch | never | 0 flinches in 3000 hits (perfectline 9.8%) |
| Mold Breaker against Sturdy | Sturdy does not hold | Sturdy holds | a full 100 HP Sturdy target kept 1 HP (perfectline: 0) |
| Focus Band, Big Root, Bug Bite and Pluck's berry, Pursuit's double power, Struggle's recoil, Magnitude's rolled power | as in singles | absent (each is a perfectline-only branch); Magnitude reads its base row | perfectline.attack and _turn hold these branches, fightsim.attack does not |

Fixing these means giving run_doubles perfectline's attack and status_move, or copying the missing branches into fightsim's.
