# Simulator gaps

This lists every move effect, ability and held item that Oxide's fight simulator does not run, or runs differently from Oxide's engine, as the scorer's readings meet them. It was checked on 2026-10-13 against origin/oxide at 93ad737260, with the simulator frozen: nothing in it was changed. It was revised on 2026-10-14 for Ian's two rulings of that day: the Z-moves and Max moves are out, and what counts as open to the player follows his rules for moves and abilities (below). Since then each row says what of it is fixed, and at which commit, as the fixes land.

## Summary

Every move in res/moves but the Z-moves and Max moves was checked through its battle effect (379 effects over 851 moves), with every ability in any species' slots (194) and every held item a trainer holds or the player can be handed (75). The 53 Z-moves and 19 Max moves (there are no G-Max moves) are out of every count and table, since Ian ruled on 2026-10-14 that they are not fixed and nobody gets them. Each was read on the readings' own path, which is perfectline's turn (its _use_move, attack, damage_of and status_move) with fightsim's switch-in and turn's end, the trainer AI and the calculator's rows. Each was then compared with Oxide's engine in src/battle and res/battle/scripts, and a small test battle settled every case where the right behaviour or the simulator's was unclear. Double battles run on fightsim's own battle instead and have a section of their own.

The gaps fall in three groups. A move is open to the player when any obtainable species has it anywhere on its level-up list (level 1 and the evolution moves at level 0 included, which the relearner and evolving reach, and a form's own list when its species is obtainable), from a TM placed in the game, or from a tutor; egg moves stay trainer-only, since there is no breeding. An ability is open when any obtainable species has it in any of its three slots, since the game's one Ability Patch reaches slot 3. Trainers may be given any move or ability on purpose, so the last group holds only what no trainer carries today and no player can reach.
