# Platinum Oxide: species pick-list

Resolved 2026-09-15 with Ian. This closes the "species pick-list" open item in
the design doc and the tracker. Generated by `tools/build_pick_list.py` from
`New Pokedex.xlsx`; the machine-readable companion is `notes/species-pick-list.csv`.

## The decision

The 360-entry curated dex is an **availability list, not a deletion list**.
All 493 Platinum natives stay in the source tree. The pick-list governs what the
regional dex shows and in what order, what appears in encounter tables, and what
the player can obtain. Nothing gets deleted, because 488 base-ROM trainer records
are being carried over in Phase 3 and many of them use species the curated dex
omits: Cynthia's Gastrodon, Roark's Geodude, Fantina's Mismagius, the entire
Chimchar line. Leaving a species present but unobtainable costs one personal
record you already have; deleting it costs trainer rewrites, script edits and a
dex renumber.

National Dex IDs stay as the internal species IDs, so Snivy is 495 in the source
tree and the donor's index-equals-dex-plus-50 rule keeps working for every import.
The 360 ordering is a dex presentation layer on top, not a renumbering.

Toxel and Toxtricity were dropped because Toxtricity's Amped/Low Key split needs
nature-conditional evolution, which Gen 4 has no method for, and Toxel is useless
without it.

established the convention: a trade-with-item evolution becomes level-up-holding-
that-item, entered twice as method 18 (day) and method 19 (night) so it fires at

friendship at night, and level-up-knowing-move, the last being how Piloswine
becomes Mamoswine).

Branch points worth noting: Yamask splits into Cofagrigus (level 34) or Runerigus
(holding Reaper Cloth); Wooper splits into Quagsire (level 20) or Clodsire
(holding Poison Barb); Goomy splits into Sliggoo (level) or Hisuian Sliggoo
(holding Metal Coat); Charcadet splits on Sun Stone or Dusk Stone; Scyther now
has three routes, Scizor by Metal Coat and Kleavor by Hard Stone, alongside the
dead trade entry.

## Leftover trade entries

The base ROM did not remove its trade evolutions, it added non-trade routes
beside them. Seventeen trade entries (methods 5 and 6) are still in the table as
unreachable dead weight. Harmless in single player. Stripping them is optional and
would free evolution slots on Clamperl, which is at six of seven.
