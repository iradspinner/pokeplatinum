split (cap 33) and Maylene's (cap 39), so that none is ridiculously trivial.
This page is that pass, on the branch `overseer-split-teams`.

## Summary

**Outcome.** 82 trainers were checked, 43 in Fantina's split (two of them
in rooms that connect to nothing) and 39 in Maylene's. 27 were rebuilt because they fell under the bar below: one in
Fantina's split (Artist William) and 26 in Maylene's, nearly all of them
the base ROM's one- and two-Pokemon teams. Two more teams in Maylene's split
kept their members and had one rule each fixed. 26 trainers are thin but
were left alone (Leonard among them, after his fix), and the rest stand as
they are. No boss and no hand-designed
team was touched.

**The bar.** A team counts as ridiculously trivial, and is rebuilt, if any
one of these holds. The same three tests went over every team.

1. Fewer than three Pokemon face the player. Two trainers who fight
   together as a tag pair count as one fight: the five Wayward Cave pairs
   met beside Mira, and the two Veilstone grunts met beside Lucas or Dawn.
   A double battle on one trainer's file counts only its own members.
2. Its strongest member sits more than eight levels under the cap in force
   where the player meets it. Those caps are 27 in the Eterna building, 30
   before Lucas and Dawn 2 and 33 after, and 39 throughout Maylene's split.
   Eight levels is the bottom of the Kaizo study's band for ordinary
   trainers.
3. Half or more of its members carry no attack of 60 effective power or
   more, counting a same-type move at one and a half times its power. A
   team like that gives a box Pokemon nothing it has to respect.

A team that passes all three is thin when it still has a gap against the
rulings, such as members without their own item and nature, an illegal or
dead move, or a weak lead. Thin teams are listed, not changed.

## Found on the way

  species, saying the species is male only. Its test is
  `ratio.endswith("MALE_ONLY")`, which `GENDER_RATIO_FEMALE_ONLY` also
