# Status moves by value to the player

Ian's reference for how much a status move is worth in the player's hands
(2026-09-27): a Generation 9 tier list he agrees with almost entirely,
transcribed from its image
([source](https://tiermaker.com/list/pokemon/pokmon-status-moves-gen-9-282544/4683605)).
It rates vanilla Generation 9 competitive play, so where Oxide's version of a
move differs (setup moves at 1 to 3 PP, the moves Oxide has not written, the
moves cut from every learnset), read its tier with that difference in mind.

Ian does not endorse two groups of placements, which count as unrated here:
the instant-death moves (Revival Blessing, Destiny Bond, and the like) and
every entry hazard other than Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web (so Stealth Rock
and Spikes).

Two cautions for Oxide. Weather moves appear here at B, but no player
learnset may teach one (the standing rulings). Dark Void sits at D because
Generation 9 made it Darkrai's alone and less accurate; Oxide's Dark Void keeps
its Generation 4 accuracy, so read it higher.
